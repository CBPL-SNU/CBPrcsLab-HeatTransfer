How T5, T10 and a bead enter the balance

Tbead−T5= hrhc (T10−Tbead)

T10 is the hot wall, T5 the air before it is heated, T6–T8 the beads between them. A bead settles where the radiation it takes from the wall equals what convection carries off, so it reads above T5 by the ratio of the two coefficients.
