Bead material and effective heat capacity

For each spherical bead, C = ρ cₚ πd³/6. Leave the effective capacity blank to use density and specific heat, or enter a calibrated capacity directly. An override takes precedence over density and specific heat. Capacity changes the response speed, not the steady-state temperature.

Air properties
Constant air properties

Automatic mode: dry ideal air at 101325 Pa; Sutherland viscosity and conductivity, constant cₚ = 1006 J/(kg·K). This is an engineering approximation, restricted here to evaluation temperatures of 250–600 K. No online lookup. Manual mode keeps k, ν and Pr constant. Bead heat capacity is a separate property.

Sutherland coefficient source. Automatic mode is opt-in; independent validation of this implementation is pending.

Model and assumptions

Wall temperature, air temperature and air velocity remain constant during the run. Each bead has a uniform temperature; conduction along the wires is excluded.

Some material properties, dimensions and emissivities are example values. Check these inputs and assumptions before comparing with measurements. Temperatures in the radiation term are absolute temperatures (K).
