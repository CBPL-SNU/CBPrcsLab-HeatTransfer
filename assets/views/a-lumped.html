Calibrate the effective heat capacity and additional heat loss coefficient UA against measured temperature histories. Convection uses the McAdams correlation.

Some material properties, dimensions and emissivities are example values. Check these inputs and assumptions before comparing with measurements. Temperatures in the radiation term are absolute temperatures (K).
